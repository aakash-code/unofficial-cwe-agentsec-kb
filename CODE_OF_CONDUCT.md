# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and
deliberately unsafe security guidance are not acceptable.

Assume good intent, discuss evidence rather than people, protect sensitive
information, and respect coordinated disclosure. Report concerns privately to
the maintainers. Maintainers may warn, edit, remove content, or restrict
participation to protect contributors and users.
